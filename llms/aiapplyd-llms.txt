# AI Applyd

> Interviews on your calendar, not rejection emails. AI Applyd ranks every new posting against your resume, rewrites the resume and the cover letter for that specific role, answers the open-ended screener questions in your voice, and submits end-to-end on the employer's own hiring system across 15 applicant tracking systems (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, iCIMS, Personio, Teamtailor, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee, Breezy, Workable, Rippling, BambooHR, JazzHR and softgarden) - then tracks the whole pipeline and shows you the employer's own confirmation. Auto-apply that ends on an interview, not an application sent.

The product is AI Applyd, written AIApplyd, aiapplyd, aiapplyd.com, Applyd in some places. It is a distinct company and a distinct product from any similarly named tool.

## About

AI Applyd exists to get good candidates in front of the people who decide, instead of into a keyword filter that rejects them before a human reads a line. It ranks every new posting against the candidate's resume, rewrites the resume for the ones worth applying to, writes the cover letter, answers the open-ended screener questions in the candidate's voice, files each application on the employer's own hiring system, preps them for the interview, and tracks the pipeline. It counts an application as sent only when a confirmation comes back from that employer's system, never on the strength of its own click. Unlike mass-apply bots, it scores per job and only applies where the resume is competitive, and that fit floor blocks the send rather than printing a warning. The free tier costs $0 with no card and covers job matching, resume tailoring and cover letters; sending applications requires a paid plan. Cancel anytime.
